Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2025

Overview

LensLogic ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use LensLogic and how it is handled.

Photos You Upload

Photos you upload are transmitted to our server solely for the purpose of generating AI feedback. We do not store, save, or retain your photos at any point. Once the analysis is returned to your browser, the image data is discarded. We have no database of uploaded images.

AI Processing

Your photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the analysis. Anthropic may process image data in accordance with their own privacy policy and terms of service. We encourage you to review Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your uploaded photos
  • The AI analysis results generated for you
  • Any payment information (the service is free)

Technical Logs

Our server may temporarily log standard request information such as IP addresses and request timestamps for the purpose of rate limiting abuse and maintaining service stability. These logs are not linked to your identity and are not retained beyond normal server operation periods.

Cookies

LensLogic does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics services. No tracking of any kind is performed.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate LensLogic:

  • Render — hosting provider for our server
  • Anthropic — AI model API for photo analysis
  • Google Fonts — typography loaded in your browser

Each of these services operates under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

LensLogic is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the service in a way that violates this policy, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of LensLogic after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at lenslogic@proton.me.
